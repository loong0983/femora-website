# FEMORA 网站图片 Prompt（给 ChatGPT 生成）

一共 7 张图。生成后**按下面的文件名命名**（.jpg），发回给我或放进网站的 `img/` 文件夹，网站会自动显示，不需要改代码。

**使用方法**

1. 先在 ChatGPT 开一个新对话，把下面「统一风格」那段贴进去，让它记住。
2. 第 1 张和第 7 张需要**先上传你的真实产品照片**（盒子正面，清楚、无反光），否则 ChatGPT 会乱画包装。
3. 每张图单独发一个 prompt。不满意就说 "same composition, but …" 让它改。
4. 图片里**不要出现任何文字、标志、认证章**（KKM / Halal / GMP 标志必须用真实的，不能让 AI 画）。

## 统一风格（先贴这段）

```
I'm creating a set of photos for a Malaysian women's herbal brand called FEMORA. Keep one consistent art direction across every image I ask for:

- Style: realistic editorial product photography, shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft warm window light from the left, gentle natural shadows.
- Colour palette: deep mangosteen plum (#3d1535) as the dominant backdrop, with accents of antique gold, deep leaf green and a touch of rose-magenta. No bright white studio backgrounds.
- Props and setting: traditional Malay materials — dark songket fabric with gold thread, batik cloth, aged teak wood, brass trays, a stone mortar and pestle, woven rattan. Elegant and calm, not cluttered.
- Strictly no text, letters, logos, labels, watermarks, badges or certification marks anywhere in the image.
- No medical imagery (no pills on white, no doctors, no anatomy), no before/after, no nudity.
```
